# OdentAI Segment — model weights

The model weights for the **OdentAI Segment 1.x** Blender add-on, distributed
separately from the add-on, under their own licence.

— non-commercial use only.** The full terms, the data source and the papers
to cite are in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Download

[`OdentAI-weights-1.zip`](https://github.com/OdentStudio/OdentAI-weights/releases/tag/weights-1)
(about 490 MB) — the four model files and the licence.

You normally do not need to download it by hand: the add-on shows the licence
and fetches the archive itself when you press **Download the model**. On a
computer without internet, download the zip here, bring it over and press
**Install from file** in the add-on. Do not unpack it.

## Data source

The weights were trained on the **ToothFairy2** and **ToothFairy3** datasets
of the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia
([ditto.ing.unimore.it](https://ditto.ing.unimore.it), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and on
**DentVoxel** (CC BY 4.0). Whenever you use these weights or results obtained
with them, acknowledge ToothFairy3 as the data source and cite the papers
listed in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

The weights may not be used for commercial purposes.

OdentAI Segment is a tool for 3D modelling and planning support, not a
certified medical device.
